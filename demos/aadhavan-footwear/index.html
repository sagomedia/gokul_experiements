The Gandhipuram Atelier

Rooted in Four Decades of Cobbling Tradition.

"We do not assemble shoes with toxic glues that break down in one monsoon. We sew each sole into the leather welt by hand, stitch by stitch."

Located right off Cross Cut Road in Gandhipuram, Coimbatore, our workshop welcomes guests to witness full-grain leather cutting, edge skiving, vegetable tanning, and custom foot mold shaping.

Read Our Full Story pin_drop Directions
Step 01

Parchment Foot Trace

Customers trace their foot contours onto archival parchment. We tailor the bridge and strap height to your exact bone structure.

Step 02

Natural Bark Immersion

Raw hides soak in extracts of acacia and babul bark for 40 days to yield our signature terracotta and saddle hues.

Step 03

Twisted Linen Hand Welt

Heavy-gauge linen thread is run through pure beeswax before being hand-stitched into the thick outer welt.

Step 04

Neem Oil Conditioning

Before boxing, every chappal receives an organic herbal oil massage to nourish the grain and resist tropical moisture.

Bespoke Fit Service

Unsure about your exact chappal size?

Trace your feet on a sheet of A4 paper, snap a top-down photo with a ruler beside it, and WhatsApp it to our master cobbler. We will recommend your precise size or craft a custom pair.
