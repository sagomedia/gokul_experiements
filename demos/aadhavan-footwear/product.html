The Bespoke Trace

How to Trace Your Foot for a Custom Fit

01

Place a blank sheet of A4 paper on a hard flat floor against a wall. Step onto it barefoot with your heel touching the wall.

02

Using a pencil held vertically at 90°, carefully trace around your foot contours from heel to longest toe and across the widest bridge.

03

Place a standard ruler alongside the paper and take a clear top-down photo with your phone.

04

Send the photo to our WhatsApp (+91 98422 15500). Our master cobbler in Gandhipuram will calibrate your exact last size before stitching.
