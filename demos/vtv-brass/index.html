The Tirunelveli Tradition

The anatomy of bell-metal casting

We do not use high-pressure hydraulic stamping. Every vessel is poured molten and hammered to acoustic resonance.

01

Virgin Ingot Smelting

Pure electrolytic copper and Grade-A tin ingots are melted together at 1050°C in charcoal crucible furnaces without zinc or toxic scrap impurities.

02

Synchronized Anvil Planishing

Three blacksmiths hammer the red-hot metal bowl in rhythmic cadence, condensing the crystalline grain structure for extreme density and acoustic ring.

03

Pure Eeyam (Tin) Swabbing

Molten virgin tin is wiped by hand using sea-sponge and sal-ammoniac flux, forming a brilliant mirror-silver food-safe seal against acidic curries.

Knowledge & Maintenance

Care guide and technical questions

Clear answers on lead-free testing, cleaning natural patina, and safely cooking traditional tamarind gravies.

Pure bell metal (*Vengalam*) is safe for general cooking, but high-acid preparations (tamarind, tomato, lemon) should be cooked in vessels with pure food-grade silver-tin lining (*Eeyam poosal*). Our brass kadais and paanais come lined with virgin tin specifically to neutralize acids.

Use traditional tamarind paste mixed with coarse rock salt or Pitambari powder. Gently scrub with a soft coconut coir or sponge, rinse with warm water, and immediately wipe dry with a clean cotton towel to prevent water spots.

Yes. We never use scrap copper wires or melted plumbing brass. Every batch is cast strictly from fresh primary copper ingots and pure tin ingots, certified 0% lead and safe for lifelong daily consumption.

Under regular daily home cooking, our thick hand-swabbed tin layer lasts 18 to 24 months. When the golden brass starts peeking through the silvery tin, any local coppersmith or our Tirunelveli workshop can re-tin it within an hour.

Every vessel is wrapped in corrugated bubble padding, placed into rigid high-density cardboard containers, and for parcels over 15 kg, reinforced with exterior wooden banding crates. We guarantee 100% replacement in case of transit damage.

Natural bronze and brass are non-magnetic, making them engineered for gas stoves, open firewood, and electric coil stoves. For induction hobs, an induction converter interface plate is recommended.

Wholesale per-kilogram billing begins at a minimum total weight of 30 kg across mixed items (e.g. Kadais, Paanais, Urulis, Lamps). For retail stores and export consolidators, custom brand stamping is also supported.
